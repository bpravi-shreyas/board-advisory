EXECUTIVE PERSPECTIVE

Five questions a CEO should ask about enterprise AI.

  1. Which revenue, customer or operating outcomes will AI materially change?
  2. What common platform capabilities will reduce duplication and accelerate adoption?
  3. How will AI-native products create defensible differentiation, rather than isolated features?
  4. How will we measure productivity and quality against credible baselines?
  5. What autonomy, data, security and human-oversight controls must scale with AI adoption?
