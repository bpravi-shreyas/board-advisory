Five questions I believe boards should ask about AI

  1. Which business outcomes justify the AI investment, and who is accountable for them?
  2. What decisions or actions can AI systems take autonomously, and where must humans approve?
  3. How are data access, security, model risk and auditability governed across the AI lifecycle?
  4. What do unit economics, operational resilience and vendor concentration look like at scale?
  5. How will the board independently assess realised value rather than deployment activity?
